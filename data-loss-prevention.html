BEFORE YOU BUY

Control how sensitive information leaves your business.

Requirements to gather

  • Identify the sensitive data, owners and business processes involved.
  • List channels: cloud applications, browser uploads, email, USB and other endpoint transfers.
  • Check operating systems, managed and unmanaged devices, remote users and existing agents.
  • Define acceptable exceptions, employee communication and who reviews policy incidents.

Define the right problem

DLP prevents or detects unauthorized data movement. Backup and disaster recovery restore lost or unavailable data. One does not replace the other.

COMPARE THE APPROACH

Choose by fit, existing systems and operating effort

Compare coverage of the channels you actually need. Evaluate Forcepoint for a multi-channel DLP requirement, Netskope where cloud and web movement is central, and Endpoint DLP Plus for an endpoint-focused requirement. Validate exact operating-system and channel support before deciding.

ManageEngine Endpoint DLP Plus

Choose to address: Control sensitive data on devices.

Suitable situation: Consider it for an endpoint-focused leakage requirement with device ownership and policy reviewers identified.

Check against existing systems: Verify supported endpoint OS versions and the channel support matrix rather than assuming uniform controls.

Budget drivers: Protected endpoints, required controls, deployment infrastructure, policy tuning and incident-review effort.

Boundary: Endpoint enforcement does not automatically cover all cloud-to-cloud or unmanaged-device activity.

Netskope One DLP

Choose to address: Reduce sensitive-data leakage.

Suitable situation: Consider it when cloud and web data movement is central and the organization can assign owners for classification and exceptions.

Check against existing systems: Check application, OS, file and device support for each proposed channel and deployment mode.

Budget drivers: Users, channels, subscription scope, endpoint rollout, steering changes and policy-review work.

Boundary: A channel must be supported, deployed and licensed to be protected.

Forcepoint DLP

Choose to address: Prevent unauthorized data movement.

Suitable situation: Consider it when required channels are documented and there is a team to tune policies, review incidents and approve exceptions.

Check against existing systems: Use the release-specific system, endpoint and file-support documentation for the selected design.

Budget drivers: Protected population, channels, infrastructure, integration, classification tuning and ongoing review.

Boundary: Channel coverage depends on components, deployment and subscription scope.

Compare licences at equivalent scope. Keep compatible systems where they meet the requirement, and use a representative pilot to verify the shortlisted configuration. Detailed capabilities and FAQs are in the linked product guides.

FEATURES, BUDGET AND SUPPORT

Compare the complete scope.

Fit with the existing environment

Bring the current product names, versions, licences and renewal dates. Check supported integrations and the effort needed to migrate, operate and maintain each option.

Users or endpoints, channel coverage, edition, subscription term, hosting and support affect scope. Include policy tuning and incident-review time; a low licence cost does not remove the effort required to operate DLP.

What a useful evaluation should establish

  • Test data classification against representative, non-confidential sample files.
  • Run an observation phase and review false positives with business owners.
  • Pilot blocking, approved exceptions and incident workflows before expanding coverage.
