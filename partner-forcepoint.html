A BALANCED DECISION

Strengths and purchase considerations

Practical strengths

  • A data-focused evaluation can address several leakage paths rather than only removable media.
  • Related web controls can be assessed where web traffic is a significant data channel.

Check before purchasing

  • Confirm endpoint operating systems, supported applications and channel coverage for the chosen deployment.
  • Plan ownership for policy tuning, incident review and approved business exceptions.
  • Start with observation and a controlled pilot before broad blocking; classification quality affects everyday work.

BUDGET AND SCOPE

What affects the cost?

Users or endpoints, protected channels, selected components and deployment model affect licensing and infrastructure. Include policy design, rollout and the agreed support scope.

Scale
Confirm the applicable device, user, workload or capacity count, including expected growth.
Edition & features
Separate essential capabilities from optional modules and add-ons.
Subscription term
Review the initial term, renewal date and any existing contracts that overlap.
Deployment
Include hosting, integration, migration and rollout work where required.
Support
Identify vendor entitlements and the ITSIPL support scope separately.

ITSIPL provides a requirement-based quotation. No indicative price is shown because the correct configuration and service scope must be established first.
