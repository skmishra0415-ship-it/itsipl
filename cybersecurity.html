COMPARE THE APPROACH

Choose by fit, existing systems and operating effort

Begin with one defined control objective. Evaluate endpoint products for endpoint threats, network products for traffic control, and DLP for sensitive-data movement. Combine controls where the requirements justify them; do not rank unrelated products against one another.

Sophos Endpoint

Choose to address: Protect computers and servers.

Suitable situation: Consider it when endpoint prevention is the immediate gap and you can nominate someone to review device health and detections.

Check against existing systems: Check exact OS versions and feature differences before selecting workstation and server coverage.

Budget drivers: Covered devices, server scope, subscription level, agent migration and the agreed operational support.

Boundary: No endpoint product guarantees that every attack will be stopped or every file recovered.

Palo Alto Networks NGFW

Choose to address: Inspect and control network traffic.

Suitable situation: Consider it when application-aware policy is a priority and your team can maintain routing, identity and inspection exceptions.

Check against existing systems: Compare hardware and software form factors against workload location, interfaces and capacity; check the chosen release support matrix.

Budget drivers: Inspection load, appliance or software size, resilience, subscription scope, logging and migration effort.

Boundary: Encrypted inspection needs certificate planning and exceptions for incompatible applications.

CrowdStrike Falcon Prevent

Choose to address: Prevent endpoint threats.

Suitable situation: Consider it when prevention is the immediate requirement and your team can administer policies and review detections.

Check against existing systems: Check supported workstation and server OS versions, deployment permissions and connectivity requirements.

Budget drivers: Endpoint and server counts, chosen package, integration work, migration and separately scoped support.

Boundary: Prevention is different from the full investigation and response scope of Falcon Insight XDR.

Netskope One DLP

Choose to address: Reduce sensitive-data leakage.

Suitable situation: Consider it when cloud and web data movement is central and the organization can assign owners for classification and exceptions.

Check against existing systems: Check application, OS, file and device support for each proposed channel and deployment mode.

Budget drivers: Users, channels, subscription scope, endpoint rollout, steering changes and policy-review work.

Boundary: A channel must be supported, deployed and licensed to be protected.

Forcepoint DLP

Choose to address: Prevent unauthorized data movement.

Suitable situation: Consider it when required channels are documented and there is a team to tune policies, review incidents and approve exceptions.

Check against existing systems: Use the release-specific system, endpoint and file-support documentation for the selected design.

Budget drivers: Protected population, channels, infrastructure, integration, classification tuning and ongoing review.

Boundary: Channel coverage depends on components, deployment and subscription scope.

Compare licences at equivalent scope. Keep compatible systems where they meet the requirement, and use a representative pilot to verify the shortlisted configuration. Detailed capabilities and FAQs are in the linked product guides.

FEATURES, BUDGET AND SUPPORT

Compare the complete scope.

Fit with the existing environment

Bring the current product names, versions, licences and renewal dates. Check supported integrations and the effort needed to migrate, operate and maintain each option.

Allow for prevention and investigation features, log retention, integrations and the people needed to operate the tools. Reuse suitable existing systems and make replacement dates explicit.

What a useful evaluation should establish

  • A written list of protected assets and excluded systems.
  • A pilot using representative devices and business applications.
  • Clear ownership of alerts, exceptions and escalation.
