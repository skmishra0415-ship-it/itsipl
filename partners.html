Make the quote clear

As your reseller and solution partner, ITSIPL helps with product selection and purchasing. The quote should identify the edition, quantities, term, vendor support and separately scoped services.

The existing ITSIPL portfolio includes solution design, implementation and ongoing support. Confirm the tasks, delivery owner, prerequisites and support boundaries for your project in the proposal.
