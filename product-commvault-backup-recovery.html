YOUR REQUIREMENT

Where this product fits

Business problems addressed

  • Different workload backup arrangements that are difficult to validate together.
  • Backups completing without evidence that priority applications can be restored.

Suitable customer situations

Consider it for a mixed on-premises and cloud estate where supported workloads and recovery dependencies can be inventoried.

Recover a representative application and document measured data loss, recovery time and unresolved dependencies.

CAPABILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

What to evaluate before purchasing

Verified capability overview

  • Backup and recovery across supported cloud and on-premises workloads.
  • Coverage for workload types such as virtual machines, databases and files.
  • Storage and deployment options to evaluate against the recovery design.

Official sources for these capabilities

Important limitations

  • Workload coverage is version- and configuration-dependent.
  • A successful backup job is not proof of a complete application recovery or a guaranteed recovery time.

Confirm the exact edition, supported versions and entitlement in your quote. Product coverage is not an ITSIPL service guarantee.

PRODUCT QUESTIONS

Commvault Cloud Backup & Recovery: common buying questions

Is backup the same as disaster recovery?

Backup supplies recoverable copies. Disaster recovery also needs a sequence, application dependencies, access and a usable recovery location.

Can we keep existing storage?

Evaluate the supported storage options and recovery performance; compatibility and capacity need confirmation.
