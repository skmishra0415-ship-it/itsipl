Sophos Firewall

YOUR REQUIREMENT

Where this product fits

Business problems addressed

  • Broad network access that needs clearer segmentation and traffic rules.
  • An existing firewall that cannot meet the intended inspection workload.

Suitable customer situations

Consider it for a branch or network refresh where routing, application flows and change windows can be documented.

Use real application flows with the intended inspection enabled; test rollback and any planned failover.

CAPABILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

What to evaluate before purchasing

Verified capability overview

  • Network traffic inspection and security policy enforcement.
  • Threat-feed-driven response and coordination with supported Sophos products.
  • Centralized management options for the firewall estate.

Official sources for these capabilities

Important limitations

  • Performance depends on inspection, encrypted traffic, sessions and the selected model.
  • A firewall does not protect data or devices whose traffic bypasses its enforcement path.

Confirm the exact edition, supported versions and entitlement in your quote. Product coverage is not an ITSIPL service guarantee.

HOW IT WORKS

From setup to everyday use

Apply rules and the licensed inspection services to allowed traffic.

Platform and deployment
