CrowdStrike Falcon Insight XDR

Falcon Insight XDR helps a security team investigate suspicious endpoint activity and take response actions. It addresses a different buying question from basic antivirus: who needs investigation context, what actions may they take, and what data should be available when an incident occurs?

Where this product fits

Business problems addressed

  • Detections without enough endpoint context for investigation.
  • A response process that cannot clearly assign containment decisions.

Suitable customer situations

Consider it when there is a named investigation owner or a separately contracted service to act on detections.

CAPABILITIES AND BOUNDARIES

What to evaluate before purchasing

Verified capability overview

  • Endpoint detection and response with contextual threat information.
  • Investigation visibility into endpoint activity.
  • Real Time Response and workflow automation; confirm the selected entitlements.

Official sources for these capabilities

Important limitations

  • A detection tool still needs an operating process and authorized responders.

PRODUCT QUESTIONS

CrowdStrike Falcon Insight XDR: common buying questions

Can I buy EDR without assigning an investigation owner?

A licence alone does not establish who investigates or authorizes response. Decide that responsibility before rollout.

Does XDR mean every data source is included?

No. Identify the specific telemetry sources, connectors and entitlements required for your environment.
