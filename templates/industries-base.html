INDUSTRY 01

Banking & Finance

Banks, finance companies and their branches handle sensitive customer and payment information every day. Customers expect services to stay available, and staff need to reach the right systems without exposing data. Your IT choices need to protect information, keep services running and allow recovery when something fails.

Three common challenges

  1. Stopping sensitive customer or financial data from leaving the organisation by mistake or misuse.
  2. Keeping branch, head-office and online systems available, with a recovery process that has been tested.
  3. Controlling how staff, branches and remote users reach internal applications.

Solution areas to look at

  • Data Loss Prevention
  • Backup & Disaster Recovery
  • Secure Access
  • Network Security

  • Palo Alto Networks (opens itsipl.com in a new tab)Next-generation firewalls with application and user-aware policy.

INDUSTRY 02

Manufacturing

Plants, warehouses and head offices rely on connected computers and networks to plan, produce and ship. When these systems stop, production can stop too. The aim is to protect them sensibly and make sure you can get back to work quickly.

Three common challenges

  1. Ransomware or malware disrupting office computers and systems that support production.
  2. Keeping networks between plants, warehouses and offices protected and reliable.
  3. Keeping many devices updated and managed across several sites.

Solution areas to look at

  • Network Security
  • Endpoint Security
  • Backup & Disaster Recovery
  • IT Infrastructure
  • Managed Services

Brands we can supply

  • Commvault (opens itsipl.com in a new tab)Backup and recovery across on-premises, cloud and SaaS workloads.

INDUSTRY 03

Healthcare

Hospitals, clinics and laboratories hold personal health information and need staff to reach systems quickly. Technology should keep records private without getting in the way of care, and systems need a plan for recovery if they become unavailable.

Three common challenges

  1. Preventing patient and personal records from being shared or exposed by mistake.
  2. Keeping clinical and administrative systems available when staff need them.
  3. Protecting many shared and mixed devices across wards, clinics and offices.

INDUSTRY 04

Education

Schools, colleges and universities support large numbers of students and staff on shared networks, often with small IT teams. Learning systems need to stay available, and student and staff information needs sensible protection.

Three common challenges

  1. Protecting a large, changing number of student, staff and shared devices.
  2. Providing safe internet and cloud access across campus and for remote learners.
  3. Backing up learning, research and administrative data with a small IT team.
