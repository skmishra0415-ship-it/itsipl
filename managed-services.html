BEFORE YOU BUY

Define the work you need supported, then choose the tools.

Requirements to gather

  • List sites, devices, applications, working hours and business-critical services.
  • Clarify what your team retains and what ITSIPL is expected to handle.
  • Identify access permissions, change approvals and escalation contacts.
  • Agree reporting, exclusions, support hours and response targets in writing.

Define the right problem

Managed IT operations and specialist security incident response are different scopes. Any monitoring hours, containment authority or incident-response service must be expressly agreed.

COMPARE THE APPROACH

Choose by fit, existing systems and operating effort

Endpoint Central, OpManager and ServiceDesk Plus address device administration, monitoring and service requests respectively. Keep existing tools if they fit the process; purchasing a tool is separate from purchasing the service to run it.

ManageEngine Endpoint Central

Choose to address: Manage employee devices.

Suitable situation: Consider it for an IT team managing a mixed or distributed device estate with defined change and maintenance windows.

Check against existing systems: Check supported OS versions and per-platform features. Compare cloud and on-premises options for your estate.

Budget drivers: Managed device counts, edition, add-ons, distribution infrastructure and deployment/support effort.

Boundary: A feature listed in the platform portfolio is not necessarily included in every edition.

ManageEngine OpManager

Choose to address: Observe infrastructure.

Suitable situation: Consider it when you can list the devices and services to monitor and nominate an owner for each alert route.

Check against existing systems: Check device models, server versions, monitoring access and the supported deployment architecture.

Budget drivers: Monitored estate, distributed sites, selected modules, hosting and the work needed to tune meaningful alerts.

Boundary: An alert does not itself restore a failed application or add capacity.

ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus

Choose to address: Organize service requests.

Suitable situation: Consider it when your team can define ticket categories, technicians, approvals and service reporting requirements.

Check against existing systems: Compare cloud and on-premises availability, hosting, backup and administrator requirements for your chosen edition.

Budget drivers: Technicians, assets, selected edition, hosting, data migration and workflow configuration.

Boundary: Installing a service desk does not establish a staffed helpdesk or an ITSIPL response commitment.

Compare licences at equivalent scope. Keep compatible systems where they meet the requirement, and use a representative pilot to verify the shortlisted configuration. Detailed capabilities and FAQs are in the linked product guides.

FEATURES, BUDGET AND SUPPORT

Compare the complete scope.

Fit with the existing environment

Bring the current product names, versions, licences and renewal dates. Check supported integrations and the effort needed to migrate, operate and maintain each option.

Costs depend on managed assets, locations, agreed activities, tooling and support coverage. Distinguish vendor product support, ITSIPL delivery work and recurring operational support rather than assuming they are one entitlement.

What a useful evaluation should establish

  • Named owners for changes and escalations.
  • An agreed asset scope and service reporting schedule.
  • A documented support window and exclusions; no implied around-the-clock commitment.

ITSIPL AS YOUR SOLUTION PARTNER

Agree the purchase and delivery responsibilities.

01 / Understand

Start with requirements

ITSIPL reviews your business need, existing systems and budget to help scope suitable technology. Bring current licences and renewal dates so a replacement is considered in context.

02 / Purchase

Make the quote clear

As your reseller and solution partner, ITSIPL helps with product selection and purchasing. The quote should identify the edition, quantities, term, vendor support and separately scoped services.

03 / Deliver

Agree responsibilities

The existing ITSIPL portfolio includes solution design, implementation and ongoing support. Confirm the tasks, delivery owner, prerequisites and support boundaries for your project in the proposal.

Product licences, vendor-operated services and ITSIPL services are separate scopes. Support hours, response targets, migration work and training are included only when specified in the proposal.
