Druva SaaS & Endpoint Backup

Druva provides cloud-delivered protection options for SaaS application content and end-user device data. This offering is relevant when the information to recover lives in employee files or collaboration services, rather than a server application that must be restarted.

Where this product fits

Business problems addressed

  • Lost employee files or collaboration content requiring recoverable copies.
  • Distributed users whose data is not consistently included in existing backup.

Suitable customer situations

Consider it when cloud delivery fits the data-location policy and the end-user or SaaS restore requirement is clearly defined.

PRODUCT QUESTIONS

Druva SaaS & Endpoint Backup: common buying questions

Are SaaS backup and endpoint backup interchangeable?

No. One protects application content and the other protects selected device data. Choose one or both according to where the recoverable information resides.

Will cloud backup work well over every connection?

Measure upload and restore times against the available bandwidth and user locations before setting recovery expectations.
